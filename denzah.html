Frequently Asked Questions

What is ZYNEARN?

ZYNEARN is a digital platform concept that combines learning, tasks, freelancing and rewards.

Is earning guaranteed?

No. Opportunities and rewards depend on available tasks, eligibility, performance and platform rules.

How do I withdraw?

The platform interface supports withdrawal requests such as M-Pesa and bank transfer. Actual payments require a secure backend integration.
